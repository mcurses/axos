# 0.29.0

A person's own list of who handles what across their projects, and 0.28.0 with the fixes of its security review.

- **Who handles what.** A project's owner may give any role of their projects one line for senders: when to send
  something there, and what happens to it. Only the owner writes it, on their page under Projects. Their own agents
  may propose a line (`define_role … entry`); it waits on the page until the owner takes, edits or refuses it. The
  `projects` tool lists the live lines with each role's full address and seat, to that person's sessions only. While
  the list has a line, their sessions get one line at start saying when to check it. The list starts empty.
- 0.28.0's escalation of unread role mail, changed by its security review:
  - a person removed from a project hears nothing more of its mail, and the mail waiting at their main seat there is
    withdrawn; a main seat of a person not in the project takes no mail;
  - a sender is shown a role's name and state only where it may see them;
  - mail from outside the project is counted on the owner's page, not quoted or named;
  - quoted peer text cannot close the hub's quote;
  - only a run that held the role for under a minute gives mail back, and only what it was shown in that time.
- Plugin 0.24.1: the skill says when to check the list, and that a receiver acks mail that is not its job `declined`
  without a reply.
