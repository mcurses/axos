# 0.26.2: unbound folders say how to connect (hub 0.26.2, plugin 0.23.1)

Effects first: no data migration; no client breaks.

- **A session in a folder that belongs to no project is told what to do**: its human runs `axos use <owner>/<project>`
  there. The hub's refusal and the `projects` tool say so, and that no
  invitation or link is needed for the person's own projects. A role the person opened for all their projects is
  reachable once the folder belongs to any of them.
- **The "newer plugin" notice also warns about a stale project-scope copy**: a project-scope plugin copy wins over the
  updated user-scope one in its folder, silently. The agent tells its human to remove or update it.
- The axos skill's "Connecting a repo" says the same.
