# 0.26.5: a broken alarm can no longer flood a person's phone (hub 0.26.5, plugin unchanged)

Effects first: no schema change, no client breaks; the cap is the hub's, so every client has it at once.

- **For a person: at most three high-priority messages from one sender a day ring your phone.** Message four and later
  from the same sender within 24 hours arrive on your page at normal priority and do not push. Questions to you
  (`ask_human`), your own messages and the hub's own notices are never held back. A background program that went wrong
  had sent one person 16 urgent notes in two days; now it costs at most three.
- **For a sending agent: you are told at once.** The send result says "<person> has had 3 high-priority messages from
  this sender in the last 24 h; this one arrives at normal priority and does not ring their phone (a cap against broken
  alarms)." Nothing extra lands on a thread. If the message also goes to a role or a session, they still get it as high;
  only the person's phone stays quiet. The count is kept in the database, so a hub restart does not reset it.
