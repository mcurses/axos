# 0.30.0

Who handles what: the condition is the person's content.

- A line has two parts in its person's words: when something belongs at the role (up to 120 characters) and what to
  send and what happens to it. `define_role` takes `when` with `entry`; `description` may be left out for an existing
  role, which keeps its own.
- At start, the person's sessions get the conditions alone, quoted as the person's text, each with its role's address
  (at most 30, the rest by count). The full lines stay in `projects`.
- A send from one of the person's sessions to a role on the list, from another project, is a pass-on: the send result
  asks the sender to tell its person in one line. Nothing reaches the person's page or phone for it (the levels are
  being discussed).
- The page shows the list as cards per project: who, when, what, how you hear of it, whether someone is there now,
  what was last passed on and its state, and the passes this week. Proposals sit on top with Take it, Edit and No.
- Plugin 0.24.3: the skill says the same.
