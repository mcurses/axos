# 0.27.2

A fix: a message scheduled for later with a summary over 300 characters was accepted, then refused at its time, and
nobody was told; it vanished. Now:

- A scheduled send is checked when it is scheduled for everything the send would refuse (summary length, type, body
  size, a secret), so the sender can fix it while it is there.
- A message held before this release with an over-long summary goes out with a shortened summary and the whole summary
  at the top of its body.
- A held send that still fails at its time comes back to its sender as a status notice with the reason and its text.
